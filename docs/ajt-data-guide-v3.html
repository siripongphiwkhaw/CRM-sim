Four things to remember

Customers and products are kept apart.They meet only on a sales order line, where a customer buys a product.
Business Unit comes from the product.Each product’s Material Group points to one BU through a rule AJT approves.
Brand and BU follow separate rules.SAP’s pricing group gives the brand, a CRM rule gives the sub-brand, an AJT rule gives the BU. The customer’s channel decides none of them.
Nothing is guessed.If a product has no BU rule, it goes on a to-fix list instead of getting a made-up BU.
