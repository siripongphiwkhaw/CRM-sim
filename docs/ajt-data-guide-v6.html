Four things to remember

Customers and products are kept apart.They meet only on a sales line, where a customer buys a product.
Every sales line gets its own BU.One order can hold several brands, so BU is never set for the whole order.
Channel first, then brand.Modern Food Service and Food Industrial customers → channel BU. Everyone else, and every consumer → the BU of the product’s brand.
B2C has its own SKU list, without a unit price.B2C SKUs are not B2B codes and are never matched to them. Four channels in Phase 1: D2C (our web shop), Shopee, Lazada, TikTok. Each listing points to one B2C SKU; the CDP sends its brand, and the brand gives the BU. A B2C line carries what the buyer paid.

B2C fields, with an example

What each source sends, and every B2C field the CRM keeps. One sale runs through all of it.

The example: a TikTok buyer orders 2 × AminoMOF promo 1+1 (TH_HYB_AJT-8850250014805_C2) and pays 398 THB. The CRM counts 4 units of AminoMOF, 99.50 THB paid per unit, BU H&N. Brand codes such as AMINOMOF are examples; the real codes come from the CRM brand list.
